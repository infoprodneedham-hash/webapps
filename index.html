Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly do I receive upon purchasing? ↓

You receive an immediate download link containing a clean ZIP archive with the complete, unminified index.html source file, a setup guide detailing how to customize the APP_CONFIG header, and your Commercial Resale License document.

Do I need coding knowledge to rebrand the apps? ↓

Minimal. Every app includes a clearly defined APP_CONFIG object at the top of the script tag. You can edit the app name, currency symbols, and default settings by simply changing text inside quotes.

Can I resell these web applications to my own customers? ↓

Yes. The included license permits you to customize, re-skin, or bundle each application for your own end-users or clients. You cannot, however, resell the exact raw template bundle on a competing digital asset marketplace as a direct clone.

How do I monetize these web apps? ↓

You can monetize through Google AdSense or affiliate network banners using the reserved advertising container slots, or use payment platforms like Stripe Payment Links or Lemon Squeezy to charge users for pro unlock keys or downloadable offline copies.
